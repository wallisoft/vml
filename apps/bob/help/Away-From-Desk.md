# Away from desk

**[Home](Home.md)** · **[Machines and SSH](Machines-and-SSH.md)**

**Away from desk** locks this computer and every Windows machine in machines.vml (back to its sign-in screen), and starts a clock. Unlock this computer and Bob logs how long you were away: every time away is kept in `~/.local/share/bob/away.csv` (date, away, back, minutes), ready for a spreadsheet or an invoice.
