## 2. What we collect

- **Account details** — your username, email address and, for some services, phone number (checked by text message).
- **Payment details** — taken by Stripe. We receive a payment reference, never your card details.
- **Licence details** — for paid licences: your name, email address and licence key.
- **Device details** — for the TinySMS app only: device ID, model, Android version, push token and SIM network.
- **Technical details** — IP addresses and web server logs, kept for security.
- **What you put on our services** — your websites, files, email and messages, kept for you and not looked at by us.

We don't collect anything about you for advertising, and we don't build profiles of you.

## 3. Product by product

### Visualised

Visualised runs on your computer. Your projects stay there unless you choose to publish them. It contacts GitHub to check for updates and download them, and to show the latest help; GitHub sees your IP address when it does. Licence keys are checked on your computer, without contacting us.

Apps made with Visualised can use three of our services, only if their maker chooses to:

- **Link** passes messages between two linked apps. They are encrypted on the sending device and can only be opened by the other one: we can't read them. We hold each message only until it's collected, and never for more than a day.
- **Sign in** with Google or GitHub: the provider tells us your name, email address and picture, and we pass them to the app you're signing in to. We don't use them for anything else.
- **Push notifications** are delivered through Google Firebase.

An app's maker is responsible for what their app does with your data. Their own privacy policy applies to it.

### TinySMS

The TinySMS app needs permission to send, receive and read text messages, because that is its job: it turns your phone into a text message gateway for your account. It doesn't touch your contacts, camera, microphone or location.

It sends us the device details listed above, and your phone number to check your SIM and route replies. We never share your number.

Messages pass through our server on their way to and from your phone. If you use TinyText (tinytext.us), your conversations are kept in your account so you can read them back; only you can see them. We count messages for billing, but we don't read or analyse them.

You can manage which senders are allowed at [tiny-web.uk/dashboard/sms](https://tiny-web.uk/dashboard/sms).

### TinyHost, TinyVPS and TinyMail

Your websites, files, containers and email are yours. We store them to provide the service, and don't look at, scan or share them. We have no access inside your containers.

Visits to websites we host are logged, as for our own sites. TinyHost counts visits so we can show each site's share of visitors; individual visitors aren't identified. Your site appears in our public showcase only if you choose.

### TinyRDP

For security, we record who connected, from which IP address and when. The session itself goes directly between the two computers (WebRTC) and isn't recorded.

### TinyDNS

We store your domain names, their IP addresses and their Cloudflare record IDs. We don't keep logs of DNS lookups.

### tiny-talk

tiny-talk has no accounts and no tracking. Messages are encrypted on your phone and can only be opened by the person you linked with. We hold them only until they're collected, and never for more than a day.

### Mountie and Juska

Mountie and Juska run on your own computers and connect directly to the servers and computers you choose. Nothing you share or transfer with them passes through us. If you buy a licence, we keep the licence details listed above.

## 4. Why we use it

- To provide our services, and to keep them working and improve them
- To take payments and issue licences
- To deliver your text messages, email and notifications
- To send you service and billing messages
- To answer you when you contact us
- To prevent abuse and keep our systems secure
- To meet our legal obligations

We never sell, rent or share your personal data for marketing. Ever.

## 5. Our legal basis

- **Contract** — to provide the service you've signed up for
- **Legitimate interests** — security logs, preventing abuse, and improving our services
- **Legal obligation** — keeping financial records as HMRC requires

## 6. Who else is involved

- **Stripe** — takes payments. We receive a confirmation and a customer reference.
- **Hetzner Online GmbH** — our servers, in the EU (Germany). Hetzner processes data under the GDPR.
- **Cloudflare** — DNS, and protection from attacks. Traffic to our websites passes through Cloudflare's network.
- **Google Firebase** — push notifications, including waking the TinySMS app. Only what's needed to deliver each notification is sent.
- **GitHub** — downloads, updates, help pages and these legal pages.
- **Google and GitHub** — if you choose to sign in with them.

Each has its own privacy policy. We share only what each needs to do its job.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Payment and licence records** — 7 years, as HMRC requires
- **Your account, and what you keep in it** — while your account is open; deleted within 30 days of you asking us to close it
- **TinySMS device records** — while the device is linked; removed when you unlink it or close your account
- **Message counts** — 90 days
- **Link and tiny-talk messages** — until collected, and never more than a day
- **Web server logs** — 30 days
- **Emails with us** — 2 years after we last hear from you

## 8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

- see the personal data we hold about you;
- have it corrected;
- have it deleted, except where the law says we must keep it;
- object to how we use it;
- receive a copy to take elsewhere;
- complain to the ICO at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

To use any of these rights, email [hello@tiny-web.uk](mailto:hello@tiny-web.uk). We reply within 30 days.

## 9. Security

- Everything sent to and from our services is encrypted (TLS 1.2 or 1.3).
- Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes, never as plain text.
- Only the systems that need personal data can reach it.
- Our servers are protected by firewalls, fail2ban and Cloudflare.
- We keep only what we need.

If you think your data has been exposed, tell us straight away at [hello@tiny-web.uk](mailto:hello@tiny-web.uk).

## 10. Cookies and browser storage

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. When you sign in to manage your account, a session cookie keeps you signed in; it's needed for the site to work, and it ends when you close your browser.

Visualised apps that run in a web browser can keep their settings in your browser's storage. That stays on your device and isn't sent to us.
