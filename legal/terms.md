## 12. Product notes

### Visualised

Visualised checks for updates and downloads them from GitHub (github.com/wallisoft). A Pro or Enterprise licence key is checked on your computer, without contacting us.

### TinySMS

TinySMS sends and receives texts through your own phone and SIM. Your mobile network's charges apply, and you're responsible for having the consent of the people you text.

### tiny-talk

tiny-talk is free. It has no accounts, adverts or tracking. It is not an emergency service: if someone is in danger, call the emergency services. Translations between pictures and languages can be imperfect, so check you've been understood when it matters.

tiny-talk uses Blissymbolics but is independent of, and not connected to, Blissymbolics Communication International (BCI).

### Mountie and Juska

Mountie and Juska run on your own computers and connect to the servers and computers you choose. We aren't part of those connections.
