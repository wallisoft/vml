# Visualised tutorials

Step-by-step guides to building apps with Visualised. Each one does a single job from start to finish, with everything you need to copy, and a check at the end that it worked. Every example has been run on the real engine.

New to Visualised? Start with **[How To Build Your First Visualised App](How-To-Build-Your-First-Visualised-App)**.

Looking something up? The **[Reference](Reference)** lists every [control](Reference-Controls), every [`Vml()` command](Reference-Commands) and the [command line](Reference-Command-Line). In the designer, **F1** opens the entry for the selected control.

## Getting started

- [How To Build Your First Visualised App](How-To-Build-Your-First-Visualised-App): a tip calculator: a form, a button and a few lines of Lua.
- [How To Build an App with Several Forms](How-To-Build-an-App-with-Several-Forms): a project with two forms and a shared module.
- [How To Debug Your Scripts](How-To-Debug-Your-Scripts): every call your scripts make, their variables, and breakpoints to step through them, VB-style.
- [How To Script in Python and C#](How-To-Script-in-Python-and-CSharp): scripts in Python (with your pip packages) and C#, kept as Lua is, and debugged the same way.
- [How To Show Help and Documents with Markdown](How-To-Show-Help-and-Documents-with-Markdown): help, notes and terms inside your app, and an editor with a live preview.

## Making it look good

- [How To Let People Pick Dates, Colours and More](How-To-Let-People-Pick-Dates-Colours-and-More): pickers for colours, dates, times and numbers, switches, a calendar, suggestions and links.
- [How To Style Controls with CSS](How-To-Style-Controls-with-CSS): shared styles, a control's own CSS, shadows, gradients and hover colours.

## Sharing your app

- [How To Put Your App on the Web](How-To-Put-Your-App-on-the-Web): one HTML file that runs in any browser.
- [How To Turn Your App into an Android App](How-To-Turn-Your-App-into-an-Android-App): an APK of its own, signed with your key.
- [How To Ship Updates to Your Users](How-To-Ship-Updates-to-Your-Users): tell users about new versions and install them, from GitHub or your own site.

## Connecting apps

- [How To Link Two Apps with Encrypted Messages](How-To-Link-Two-Apps-with-Encrypted-Messages): a chat between two phones, end to end encrypted, in about 60 lines of Lua.
